How many rooms on each floor?

Shell n contains n subshells. Each subshell adds two more orbitals than the one before (1, 3, 5, 7), and every orbital holds two electrons. Tap a floor to see its totals.

Patterns to notice

Madelung's rule

Why 4s fills before 3d

In hydrogen, with only one electron, every subshell in a shell has the same energy. With more electrons, they shield each other and the subshells split apart. Some even pass subshells from the shell above. Madelung's rule predicts the order: lowest n + ℓ first, and if two tie, lower n first.

Dashed levels (5s, 5p, 6s) are beyond n = 4 but fill before 4d and 4f.

Adding electrons one at a time

Three rules place each new electron. Aufbau: take the lowest-energy subshell with room. Pauli: at most two per orbital, with opposite spins. Hund: within a subshell, fill every orbital singly before pairing.
