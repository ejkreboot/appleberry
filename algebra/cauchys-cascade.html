Cauchy's Cascade

Each falling bar is an equation; each column is a letter. Multiply the bar (or the stack) so a column matches, and when it lands that letter pops. Get a row down to one letter and it's solved. Pops can tumble into the rows below.

How to make a match
  1. Same number: just let it drop.
  2. Same number, opposite sign (4 and −4): flip the bar with ↑.
  3. One goes into the other (5 and 15): multiply the smaller one. Bar: type 3. Stack: Space 3.
  4. Neither goes in (20 and 30): multiply both until they meet. 20 × 3 = 60 = 30 × 2.
  5. Rows missing the same letter are worth gold: pop one against the other and only one letter is left.
On paper
